## Quality findings

The group identified three risks: negative input, protected journeys receiving
advice, and a household total being interpreted as one person's share. It also
noticed that a low-fi prototype can still be too polished if styling hides the
conceptual decisions. The chosen visual treatment was therefore restrained,
with explicit status labels and no fabricated factor library.
