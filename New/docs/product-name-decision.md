# Product name decision

**Recommended working name:** **Footprint Lens**  
**Subtitle:** *An India-centric lens for understanding routines and realistic choices.*

## Why this name is stronger

- **Lens** communicates interpretation and transparency, matching the D1 problem
  of abstract numbers being difficult to understand.
- **Footprint** is understandable without promising that the product itself will
  reduce emissions.
- It is short enough for a JavaFX title bar, prototype header and report cover.
- The subtitle keeps the India-centric coursework challenge visible without
  narrowing the product name to one language or user group.

## Alternatives considered

| Name | Strength | Reason not selected |
|---|---|---|
| Nirmaan | Suggests constructive change | Requires explanation for users unfamiliar with Hindi/Sanskrit terminology |
| GreenSteps | Friendly and action-oriented | Implies a reduction journey before the evidence supports that claim |
| Earthwise | Clear sustainability association | Less specific to footprint interpretation and crowded as a product phrase |
| **Footprint Lens** | Transparent, calm and evidence-aligned | Retained as the review name |

The technical package/class identifiers may retain historical `carbon` names until
the team confirms whether a full repository rename is worth the migration risk.
