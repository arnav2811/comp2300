## Review rotation proposal

| Artefact type | Author/lead | First reviewer | Second perspective |
|---|---|---|---|
| Evidence/research | Arnav | Keshav | Hiranya |
| Requirements/technical | Keshav | Hiranya | Arnav |

The author must not be the sole reviewer. A review is complete only when the
comment, response, change and verification are recorded.
