## Product quality direction

Each stage increases product capability without weakening the evidence boundary:
numbers become more inspectable, choices become more feasible, and unsupported
claims remain visible. The target is a convincing and responsibly scoped
engineering artefact rather than a feature list that implies unmeasured impact.
