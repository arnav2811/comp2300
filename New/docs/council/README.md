# Three-agent review council

The council consists of three AI review personas requested by the project plan:

- **Agent Arnav:** research, strategy, editorial quality and traceability.
- **Agent Keshav:** Java/JavaFX engineering, correctness, architecture and tests.
- **Agent Hiranya:** UX, accessibility, localisation and workflow.

The re-orchestration directive required a fresh first pass. Keshav and Hiranya
returned substantive reports; both requested Agent Arnav attempts failed at the
service layer, so `agent-arnav-review.md` is explicitly an Astra fallback rather
than a fabricated independent-agent result.

They are AI reviewers, not the students, not Git identities and not Blackboard
peer reviewers. Their findings can improve artefacts but cannot establish human
attendance, approval, contribution, PRISM evidence or participant results.

## Review protocol

Each major review records independent findings, disagreement, resolution,
priority, human verification required and a verdict. The council uses three
verdicts: **ready for review**, **conditionally ready**, and **not ready**.
