## 3. Findings

### A-01 — Formal D2 evidence gates remain open — Critical

`D2/conceptual-design-working.md` explicitly says the ideation canvas is a
starting canvas and that authentic team ideation evidence, final team choice and
verified competitor comparison are pending. The report source is present but no
compiled current PDF was produced because no LaTeX compiler is installed.

**Acceptance:** team supplies/approves ideation evidence, confirms Concept A,
adds a defensible competitor/novelty comparison, compiles and visually inspects
the PDF, and records the actual final version.

### A-02 — Product novelty must remain a hypothesis — High

The trust checkpoint, unavailable state, protected-choice boundary and explicit
household/non-attribution rule form a coherent product thesis. They do not alone
prove market novelty. The current documents appropriately mark novelty evidence
as pending; that wording must survive final editing.

### A-03 — D2 story/prototype language needs one scope — High

The selected concept describes comparison and action choice, while the browser
prototype is intentionally preview-only and has no persistence. The repair pass
now clears stale quantity on a new route and labels desktop action choices as
session-only, but the final D2 wording must not imply measured behaviour change,
saved plans or participant validation.

### A-04 — India-centric claims need a source chain — High

The illustrative electricity factor and ₹ comparison are clearly marked as
fixtures, but geography, tariff period, taxes and exclusions are not verified.
The product should either remain an evidence-conscious concept demonstration or
add a source table with region, period, metric, denominator and boundary before
presenting national estimates.

### A-05 — D3–D7 are prepared frameworks, not completed empirical work — Critical

The evaluation protocol, blank schema, analysis tests and materials are useful.
No recruitment, consent, controlled session, participant observation or findings
exist. Simulated meetings, peer reviews, workload, hours and reflections remain
outside formal evidence and require human replacement.

### A-06 — Document set needs final editorial control — Medium

The workspace has grown to many handover documents. The dashboard helps, but a
formal submission should nominate one authoritative report per deliverable,
remove rehearsal-only prose from official folders and check British English,
references, captions, page count and word limits after PDF compilation.

### A-07 — Learning outcomes are useful only when personalised — Medium

The learning register has specific change-based examples, such as household
totals needing explicit scope labels and unknown not meaning zero. Individual
drafts correctly contain placeholders. They must not be submitted as actual
reflection or contribution evidence without student rewriting and confirmation.
