## Established content

The supplied report contains a literature review, thematic analysis of CF1–CF10,
six personas, four scenarios, a 62-word JTBD, epics E1–E14 and MoSCoW
prioritisation. Its central baseline is that impact information must be legible,
localised and connected to feasible actions, while shared household and safety
constraints must not be converted into blame.

## Decisions to carry forward

- Research questions RQ1–RQ3 remain the evaluation direction unless D2 records a
  justified change.
- Must epics E1–E5 form the initial core journey; E6–E9 are Should; E10–E12 are
  Could; E13–E14 are excluded for the current version in the supplied report.
- D1 explicitly labels several scenario factors as illustrative or requiring
  verification. Those values cannot become authoritative implementation data
  without a source audit.
- CF1–CF10 are treated as illustrative coursework transcripts, not a
  representative population sample.

## Evidence limitations

- The transcript files cited by the D1 report were not supplied in the workspace
  audit, so quoted content was not independently rechecked here.
- The D1 report contains unresolved `[VERIFY]` factor/source notes.
- The supplied PDF does not by itself prove Blackboard submission, peer-review
  submission or lecturer approval.

## Handover to D2

D2 must preserve stable IDs, make supporting and contradictory evidence visible,
split Must epics into testable stories, and settle the noted attribution and
