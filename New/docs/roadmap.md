# Roadmap and Thursday checkpoints

Official submission dates are taken from the supplied coursework specification.
The Thursday dates below are proposed team checkpoints beginning 29 October 2026;
they are not official deadlines and must be moved if the team agrees a change.
No row describes a meeting that has already happened unless an actual minute
record is linked.

## Checkpoint standard

At each checkpoint: review prior actions; inspect the artefact and GitLab state;
check the relevant rubric; update risks, `logic.md`, `strategy.md` and the commit
plan; agree named owners and dates; and record actual minutes using the official
template.
