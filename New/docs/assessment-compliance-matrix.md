observable artefact or executed check; `Pending human` means a student or live
course system must confirm it; `Not conducted` means no evidence exists.

## Release rule

No deliverable is marked formally complete merely because its report source exists.
The team must replace `Draft`, `Pending human`, `Pending`, or `Not conducted` with
actual evidence references and execution results before submission.
