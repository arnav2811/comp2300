# D2 rubric and acceptance matrix

| Criterion | Official requirement | Evidence required | Current evidence | Gap/next action | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Design concepts | Three distinct concepts; explain how each addresses the challenge; justify one by suitability, feasibility and stakeholder fit | Ideation canvas, `concepts-and-diagrams.md`, three concept descriptions, comparison and selection rationale | Working draft sections 3–5, diagrams and report source | Add authentic team ideation evidence, verified competitor comparison and final team choice | Draft complete / review pending |
| Small user stories | Stories grouped by epic, stakeholder, MoSCoW and acceptance criteria | Story register with stable IDs and D1 traceability | Working draft section 6; traceability register | Team confirms selected scope and blocked/partial paths | Draft complete / review pending |
| Low-fidelity prototype | Appropriate-fidelity prototype, feasible, accessible, novel and more than logging/display | Editable prototype, rendered panels, interaction inventory and walkthrough coverage | `prototype/` covers P0–P6; automated checks, scenario matrix and control inventory | Team keyboard/readability browser review and novelty evidence | Draft complete / review pending |
| Report completeness | Work summary, tasks, management techniques, tools/techniques, product/process reflection | `report.md` and `report/main.tex` | Working report and supplied Week 6/7 baseline | Replace pending placeholders with actual student records | Draft complete / review pending |
| Formatting | PDF, A4, >=12 pt, >=1.5 cm margins, title page, ACM SIGCHI citations/references | Rendered PDF and source | Official requirement recorded | Render final artefact and inspect every page | Missing |
| AI disclosure | Tool names/versions/purposes and student verification | Correct `genai.txt` submitted with artefact | Supplied disclosure exists but requires student completion/confirmation | Confirm exact tools/versions and retained content | Open |

## D2 release gate

The D2 pack is ready for submission only when every row is `Verified` by actual
evidence, the team has reviewed the content, and the final PDF/archive opens and
matches the source. A persuasive draft is not a passed gate.
