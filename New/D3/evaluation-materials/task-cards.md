# Evaluation task cards — draft

Use synthetic values and the same task order for every participant unless the
team pre-registers a counterbalancing rule.

| Task | Prompt | Completion criterion |
|---|---|---|
| T1 | Record 168 kWh of home electricity for a household and explain the result | Enters valid quantity/scope and states what the result represents |
| T2 | Find the factor and explain which details make the number trustworthy or limited | Opens assumptions and identifies status, geography or boundary |
| T3 | Record a night-shift journey as protected and look for an action | Protected record remains visible without punitive advice |
| T4 | Record delivery packaging for reuse without a supplied factor | Keeps a descriptive record and recognises estimate-unavailable behaviour |
| T5 | Inspect the household total and explain whether it is one person's share | States that a household total is not individual attribution |

The moderator records completion, critical error, help request, comprehension
answer, optional interaction time and a short observation. Do not coach except
to repeat the fixed prompt.
