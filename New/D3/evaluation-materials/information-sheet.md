# Footprint Lens evaluation information sheet — draft

**Study status:** material prepared for team review; no recruitment or session is
claimed.

The study explores how another COMP2300 student understands a Footprint Lens
desktop prototype that records synthetic lifestyle activities. It is not a test of the participant's
carbon footprint, lifestyle, income, health or household. Participation is
voluntary and withdrawal is allowed before de-identified analysis.

The moderator will use synthetic values. The team will not request names,
addresses, financial details, medical information, political/religious beliefs or
real household records. Notes will use a participant code only and will be stored
in the approved project evidence location. The report will describe this small
course sample and will not claim population-wide behaviour change or carbon
reduction.

Before use, the team must confirm the course consent wording, storage period,
recruitment channel, participant count decision rule and lecturer requirements.
