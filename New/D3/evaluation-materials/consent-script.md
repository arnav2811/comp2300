clarity of a Footprint Lens prototype, not you. We will use synthetic activity

happen. You may skip a question, stop at any time and ask for the task to be
repeated. We will not collect your name or sensitive personal information. Notes
will use a participant code and will be reported in aggregate/descriptive form.

- I understand the session is about prototype comprehension, not my actual
  carbon footprint.
